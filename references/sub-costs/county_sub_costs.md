# County manufactured-home sub-cost estimates

All dollar figures are estimates. They are not bids, contracts, or quotes for a specific parcel. Fee schedules, dealer prices, and contractor quotes change; confirm the current amount before relying on it. A blank low or high means that figure was not found, not that the cost is zero.

Every row is labeled `estimate`. Shared site and finish rows are statewide or generic ranges, not a county quote. Flagler call amounts that have no published source are written up in [flagler-baseline-quotes.md](flagler-baseline-quotes.md).
