# Land-home operating workflow

Use this as a dependency template, then adapt it to the property, contract, approved plans and agency requirements. Treat all proposed operating targets as internal targets until the responsible party commits.

## Start at contract

Confirm seller permission for the actual survey/testing access, who may authorize DD spending, the cost ceiling, responsibility if the purchase fails, the DD expiration and any earlier notice deadline. Assign a PM and escalation owner. Set the survey field date and signed deliverable date against the decision deadline.

Order a coordinated survey scope covering boundaries/easements/corners, necessary elevations/drainage and applicable trees; obtain prices for later staking and final work. Start septic evaluation, home-plan collection, power-service planning and site pricing in parallel. A boundary-only drawing may leave needed field evidence outstanding.

Distinguish home model selection, a conditional inventory hold, nonrefundable procurement and dispatch. Record floor plan, bedrooms, actual footprint, installation documents, availability, commitment terms and transport requirements before freezing design assumptions.

## Parallel branches and joins

| Septic site evaluation | Authorized evaluator access and proposed home information exist | Parcel-specific soils, water-table and design/elevation input |
| Home and utility planning | Contract and basic project information exist | Home availability/requirements and utility scope/lead-time evidence |
| Layout and site budget | Sufficient survey, evaluation, home and access information exists | Dated coordinated revision and cost assumptions reviewed by responsible parties |
| Purchase decision | Before the actual DD notice deadline | Requity decision, unresolved issues and accepted cost/risk recorded |
| Well/septic approvals, if applicable | Coordinated layout and applicant/signature requirements exist | Issued applicable permits; preparation can proceed concurrently |
| Remaining permit documents | Relevant design and signer information exists | Complete current packet; installer documents need not already be agency-approved just to be submitted |
| Main permit submission | All current required filing prerequisites exist | Agency submission and acceptance evidence, tracked separately |
| Sitework release | Ownership/authority and applicable work permissions exist | Issued permits, commencement documentation and other applicable start conditions verified |
| Access/pad/utilities | Work-specific release requirements exist | Field readiness and access protection documented |
| Home dispatch and set | Site, home, installer and transporter are ready | Confirmed home and crews, route/pad acceptance and actual set event |
| Trade work | Home/set and stage-specific prerequisites exist | Compatible overlap while preserving required intermediate inspections |
| GC scope completion | Agreed subcontractor scope and scope evidence are complete | PM confirmation against contract; separate from CO |
| Occupancy closeout | Applicable finals, utility and water/wastewater clearances exist | Agency occupancy/CO evidence, not a portal checkbox |

Prepare signatures and complete packages while external approvals are pending. Confirm whether each agency accepts a preclosing applicant, whose authorization is needed and how ownership updates work. One office's acceptance does not establish another's, and preparation does not authorize filing or construction.

## Release checks

- **Purchase:** surface buildability evidence, budget, missing investigations and notice deadlines for Requity's decision. Escalate extension/exit/risk decisions before the notice deadline when essential evidence is late; never infer an extension.
- **Filing:** verify parcel/applicant, current forms, signatures, layout revision and required attachments/approvals. Avoid knowingly incomplete submissions used only to reserve queue position.
- **Sitework:** identify the actual work being released and its permits. Verify ownership or separately documented authority, applicable issued permits and commencement requirements. For applicable Florida improvements, recording and posting NOC belongs before commencement, not merely before inspection scheduling.
- **Delivery:** obtain field confirmation of route, turning/swing space, overhead clearance, pad/ground readiness, section staging and crew commitments. A supplier estimate is not a dispatch confirmation.
- **Handover:** distinguish contracted GC completion from occupancy readiness; assign outstanding tests, utility activation and final approvals even if they extend beyond subcontractor completion.

Return met / unmet / unknown with evidence and as-of date. Revalidate after plan changes, expiration, site changes or material delay.

## Field access and closeout

Prefer an accessible front-corner well only when permitted constraints and trade access support it. Review neighboring wells/septic and, where controlled together, adjoining lots before settling either layout.

Drill early when the wellhead and future rig access can be protected. Obtain septic approval early; install before delivery only when trucks can avoid the system area. For installation after delivery, preserve rear equipment access and coordinate excavation/grading. Keep trenches, utilities and clearing compatible with delivery and required inspections. Leave final driveway surfacing and landscaping until heavy traffic is finished where practicable.

Start utility planning early; track readiness, electrical release, utility scheduling and energization separately. Plan sampling, septic final, reporting and inspections early enough for corrections. Do not wait for GC completion to coordinate closeout or put all inspections at the end.

## Two-week and portfolio control

Review ready work, approaching readiness, follow-ups, expiring evidence and each project's next decision. Each actionable row needs one owner, next action and date or explicit missing commitment. Reserve conditional slots with release requirements, then confirm against actual readiness.

Use vendor-confirmed capacity and availability, including travel and mobilization. Unknown capacity means a conditional appointment; do not assume one crew can serve all lots on one day. Advance inexpensive authorized DD across contracted properties while releasing construction in waves constrained trades can support.
